# Cardiac Surgery Education Series

Elysian Biomedical LLP · 20 episodes

Episodes 1–3 are the opening coronary bypass sequence. They explain the treatment decision, how a graft carries blood, and recovery. Episodes 4–20 each cover a different operation. Each new episode has English narration, a timed illustration storyboard, and a source map linking clinical claims to the scenes.
