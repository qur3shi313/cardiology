## Anatomy, continuity and safety

- The aortic valve is between the left ventricle and ascending aorta. The new valve occupies its native position; ostia stay above the valve plane.
- In the result scene, the leaflets open during left ventricular ejection and close in relaxation. Keep forward flow and leakage distinct.
- Maintain one generic tissue prosthesis from the choice scene to implantation and result. No application, cannulation, suturing or equipment settings.
- At 390-pixel phone width, use high-contrast type, no more than two live labels and a legend for forward flow versus leakage. Reframe rather than shrinking labels. Use stable anatomy on comparisons.
