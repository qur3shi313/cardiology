## Anatomy, continuity and safety

- Keep the left atrium and ventricle fixed; the posterior leaflet moves abnormally toward the left atrium during systole, not into the aorta.
- The added cords are distinct from the annular band. Both native leaflets remain; use the same torn chord position and later supported segment.
- Use separate flow cues for ventricular filling and systolic regurgitation; the animation is not an operative guide.
- At 390-pixel phone width, use high-contrast type, no more than two live labels and a legend for forward flow versus leakage. Reframe rather than shrinking labels. Use stable anatomy on comparisons.
