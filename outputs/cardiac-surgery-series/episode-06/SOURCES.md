# Clinical sources and scene map

**Surgical mitral valve replacement**

Elysian Biomedical LLP · Cardiac Surgery Education Series · Episode 06

Clinical sources checked on **13 November 2026**. This patient-facing teaching example shows the purpose and progression of one operation. The selected surgical approach and care plan vary by patient and centre. Illustrate original artwork; the references are evidence, not assets or endorsements.

## Dated references

- **A · ESC/EACTS valvular heart disease guideline, published online 29 August 2025.** Chapters on aortic stenosis, mitral and tricuspid valve disease, Heart Team decisions, valve prostheses and follow-up. [European Heart Journal full guideline](https://academic.oup.com/eurheartj/article/46/44/4635/8234488)
- **B · Mayo Clinic, Mitral valve repair and mitral valve replacement, 15 August 2026.** Institutional patient reference for mitral valve anatomy, disease, repair and replacement methods, risks and recovery. [Mayo Clinic mitral valve surgery](https://www.mayoclinic.org/tests-procedures/mitral-valve-repair-mitral-valve-replacement/about/pac-20384958)
- **C · NHS, Heart valve replacement recovery and complications, reviewed 17 July 2025.** Supports wound and activity guidance, follow-up and rehabilitation, patient-specific medicines, and urgent or emergency warning signs. [NHS recovery](https://www.nhs.uk/tests-and-treatments/heart-valve-replacement/recovery/) · [NHS complications](https://www.nhs.uk/tests-and-treatments/heart-valve-replacement/complications/)
- **D · NHS, Heart valve replacement procedure, reviewed 17 July 2025.** Supports the conventional open surgical example, temporary heart-lung support, replacement and the transition to intensive care. [NHS procedure guide](https://www.nhs.uk/tests-and-treatments/heart-valve-replacement/how-its-done/)
- **E · Mayo Clinic, Tricuspid valve repair and tricuspid valve replacement, 17 June 2026.** Institutional patient information for right-sided valve anatomy, ring or band repair, risks, pacemaker as a possibility, and recovery. [Mayo Clinic tricuspid valve surgery](https://www.mayoclinic.org/tests-procedures/tricuspid-valve-repair-tricuspid-valve-replacement/about/pac-20385087)
- **F · NHS, Shortness of breath, reviewed 30 January 2024.** Supports seeking emergency care for severe difficulty breathing. [NHS breathing and urgency](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/shortness-of-breath/)
