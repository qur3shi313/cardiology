## Anatomy, continuity and safety

- The native mitral and prosthetic valves share the annular plane between left atrium and ventricle. Do not replace or obstruct the separate aortic outflow in the illustration.
- Keep the scarred native valve distinct from the tissue replacement. Use identical prosthesis silhouette and orientation across seating, ultrasound and recovery summaries.
- Use two heart phases with persistent chamber names. No equipment, suturing, or anticoagulation technique.
- At 390-pixel phone width, use high-contrast type, no more than two live labels and a legend for forward flow versus leakage. Reframe rather than shrinking labels. Use stable anatomy on comparisons.
