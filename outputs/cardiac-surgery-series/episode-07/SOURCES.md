# Clinical sources and scene map

**Surgical tricuspid valve repair**

Elysian Biomedical LLP · Cardiac Surgery Education Series · Episode 07

Clinical sources checked on **13 November 2026**. This patient-facing teaching example shows the purpose and progression of one operation. The selected surgical approach and care plan vary by patient and centre. Illustrate original artwork; the references are evidence, not assets or endorsements.

## Dated references

- **A · ESC/EACTS valvular heart disease guideline, published online 29 August 2025.** Chapters on aortic stenosis, mitral and tricuspid valve disease, Heart Team decisions, valve prostheses and follow-up. [European Heart Journal full guideline](https://academic.oup.com/eurheartj/article/46/44/4635/8234488)
- **B · Mayo Clinic, Tricuspid valve repair and tricuspid valve replacement, 17 June 2026.** Institutional patient information for right-sided valve anatomy, ring or band repair, operative risks and recovery. [Mayo Clinic tricuspid valve surgery](https://www.mayoclinic.org/tests-procedures/tricuspid-valve-repair-tricuspid-valve-replacement/about/pac-20385087)
- **C · Gammie et al., CTSN randomized trial of concomitant tricuspid repair, published in the 27 January 2022 issue.** Supports the repair-versus-no-repair trade-off during surgery for degenerative mitral regurgitation, including increased pacemaker implantation in the combined group. No percentages are generalized. [Primary trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34767705/)
- **D · NHS, Heart valve replacement recovery and complications, reviewed 17 July 2025.** Supports wound and activity guidance, follow-up and rehabilitation, patient-specific medicines, and urgent or emergency warning signs. [NHS recovery](https://www.nhs.uk/tests-and-treatments/heart-valve-replacement/recovery/) · [NHS complications](https://www.nhs.uk/tests-and-treatments/heart-valve-replacement/complications/)
- **E · Cleveland Clinic, Pulmonary Valve Disease Care, accessed 13 November 2026; no page date shown.** Institutional pulmonary disease and treatment overview; separate valve position and later surgical replacement as a treatment approach. [Pulmonary valve care](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/services/pulmonary-valve-disease-treatment)
- **F · NHS, Shortness of breath, reviewed 30 January 2024.** Supports seeking emergency care for severe difficulty breathing. [NHS breathing and urgency](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/shortness-of-breath/)
