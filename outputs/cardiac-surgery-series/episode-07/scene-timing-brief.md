## Anatomy, continuity and safety

- Use a fixed right atrium–right ventricle orientation. Systolic leakage runs upward into the right atrium and is separate from forward pulmonary outflow.
- Keep the same native leaflets visible before and after ring support. Ring may be incomplete; no site-by-site attachment detail.
- The selected example is concomitant with mitral surgery, which is not illustrated. Show rhythm monitoring and possible pacing as different concepts.
- At 390-pixel phone width, use high-contrast type, no more than two live labels and a legend for forward flow versus leakage. Reframe rather than shrinking labels. Use stable anatomy on comparisons.
