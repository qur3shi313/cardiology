# Clinical sources and scene map

**Surgical pulmonary valve replacement after tetralogy of Fallot repair**

Elysian Biomedical LLP · Cardiac Surgery Education Series · Episode 08

Clinical sources checked on **13 November 2026**. This patient-facing teaching example shows the purpose and progression of one operation. The selected surgical approach and care plan vary by patient and centre. Illustrate original artwork; the references are evidence, not assets or endorsements.

## Dated references

- **A · ACC/AHA/HRS/ISACHD/SCAI adult congenital heart disease guideline, published online by JACC on 18 December 2025.** Section 4.3.5 addresses repaired tetralogy of Fallot, ultrasound and cardiac MRI, considerations for replacement, surgical and transcatheter options, and lifelong surveillance. [JACC full guideline](https://www.jacc.org/doi/10.1016/j.jacc.2025.09.006) · [Circulation full guideline PDF](https://www.ahajournals.org/doi/pdf/10.1161/CIR.0000000000001402)
- **B · Mayo Clinic, Pulmonary valve repair and replacement, 2 October 2026.** Institutional patient education supports native valve position, surgery using heart-lung support, replacement with a biological tissue valve, recovery and complications. [Pulmonary valve surgery](https://www.mayoclinic.org/tests-procedures/pulmonary-valve-repair-pulmonary-valve-replacement/about/pac-20385090)
- **C · NHS, Heart valve replacement recovery and complications, reviewed 17 July 2025.** Supports wound and activity guidance, follow-up and rehabilitation, patient-specific medicines, and urgent or emergency warning signs. [NHS recovery](https://www.nhs.uk/tests-and-treatments/heart-valve-replacement/recovery/) · [NHS complications](https://www.nhs.uk/tests-and-treatments/heart-valve-replacement/complications/)
- **D · Mayo Clinic, Aortic valve stenosis diagnosis and treatment, 10 May 2025.** The Ross procedure is described as replacing the aortic valve with the patient’s pulmonary valve. [Aortic valve disease treatment and Ross procedure](https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/aortic-stenosis/diagnosis-treatment/drc-20353145)
- **E · NHS, Shortness of breath, reviewed 30 January 2024.** Supports seeking emergency care for severe difficulty breathing. [NHS breathing and urgency](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/shortness-of-breath/)
