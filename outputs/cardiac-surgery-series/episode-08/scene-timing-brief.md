## Anatomy, continuity and safety

- The pulmonary valve is in the right ventricular outflow, distinct from the tricuspid inlet and the left ventricular aortic outlet. Preserve the same repaired outflow anatomy before and after placement.
- Show forward flow during ventricular ejection and pulmonary regurgitation during ventricular relaxation. The latter returns from pulmonary artery into the right ventricle, not to the right atrium.
- Use one generic tissue valve and one adult repaired congenital case; avoid specific prior repair, device eligibility, operative or medicine instructions.
- At 390-pixel phone width, use high-contrast type, no more than two live labels and a legend for forward flow versus leakage. Reframe rather than shrinking labels. Use stable anatomy on comparisons.
