# Clinical sources and scene map

**Bentall Operation: Replacing the Aortic Root and Valve**

Elysian Biomedical LLP · Cardiac Surgery Education Series · Episode 11

Clinical sources checked on **13 November 2026**. This episode presents a single illustrative pathway. Decisions, risks and recovery depend on the individual. No operative instructions are illustrated.

## Dated references

- **A · Mayo Clinic aortic root surgery, 27 November 2024.** Distinguishes valve-sparing and composite replacement and covers risks and recovery. [Aortic root surgery](https://www.mayoclinic.org/tests-procedures/aortic-root-surgery/about/pac-20383396)
- **B · ACC/AHA guideline for aortic disease, published online 2 November 2022.** Defines the aortic root and the extent and timing of repair, distinguishes valve-sparing repair from a valved conduit, and sets follow-up principles. [Full ACC/AHA guideline](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9860464/)
- **C · Cleveland Clinic aortic root replacement, updated 19 January 2026.** Explains root anatomy, composite valve and graft replacement, the coronary reconnections, recovery, and follow-up. [Aortic root replacement](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/22821-aortic-root-replacement)
- **D · ACC/AHA valve disease guideline (ACC summary), 17 December 2020.** Describes prosthetic valve surveillance and anticoagulation for mechanical valves. [Valve guideline, part 3](https://www.acc.org/latest-in-cardiology/ten-points-to-remember/2020/12/16/22/01/2020-acc-aha-vhd-gl-pt-3-gl-vhd)
