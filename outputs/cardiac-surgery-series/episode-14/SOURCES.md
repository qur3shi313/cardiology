# Clinical sources and scene map

**Cox-Maze IV surgery for atrial fibrillation**

Elysian Biomedical LLP · Cardiac Surgery Education Series · Episode 14

Clinical sources checked on **13 November 2026**. The operation illustrated is one open, biatrial Cox-Maze IV example. Rhythm control and stroke prevention remain distinct decisions.

## Dated references

- **A · Society of Thoracic Surgeons surgical AF guideline.** Wyler von Ballmoos et al., published online 27 January 2024, in the August 2024 issue. Supports surgical ablation and left atrial appendage management in defined operative contexts, multidisciplinary selection and follow-up. [STS clinical practice guideline](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38286206/)
- **B · EHRA/HRS/APHRS/LAHRS ablation consensus, 2024.** The surgical section illustrates the left atrial lesion set, posterior wall box and relationships to pulmonary veins. The 2017 STS guideline provides complementary right atrial anatomy in Figure 2. Use these illustrations as anatomical references for original artwork. Use A for current STS clinical recommendations. [2024 society consensus](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/joa3.13082) · [STS illustrated lesion sets](https://www.sts.org/sites/default/files/documents/Clinical%20Guidelines/SurgicalTreatmentAfib.pdf)
- **C · Cleveland Clinic Maze Procedure for Atrial Fibrillation, updated 6 April 2022.** Explains disordered atrial activation, biatrial lines, an open bypass-supported example, appendage management, complications, and recovery. [Cleveland Clinic procedure guide](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/17086-heart-surgery-for-atrial-fibrillation-maze)
- **D · Washington University Department of Surgery Maze Procedure, accessed 13 November 2026.** Explains symptoms, selection for standalone or concomitant surgery, ECG and ambulatory monitoring, recurrence, and postoperative risks and medicines. No page-level publication date was displayed. [WashU surgical patient guide](https://surgery.wustl.edu/patient-care/services/cardiac-surgery/maze-procedure/)
