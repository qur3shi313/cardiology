## Dated references

- **A · Saeed et al., 2023 ISHLT Guidelines for Mechanical Circulatory Support, published 25 May 2023.** Covers selection, implant care, right-sided support, follow-up and management of complications for durable systems. [ISHLT guideline](https://www.ishlt.org/education-and-publications/standards-guidelines-detail/the-2023-ishlt-guidelines-for-mechanical-circulatory-support-a-10--year-update)
- **B · Tedford et al., Durable Mechanical Circulatory Support, JACC scientific statement, published online 25 September 2023.** Supports the configuration of the internal pump, apical inflow and ascending-aortic outflow graft, and the clinical importance of right ventricular function. [JACC scientific statement](https://www.jacc.org/doi/10.1016/j.jacc.2023.07.019)
- **C · Johns Hopkins Medicine, Left Ventricular Assist Device, accessed 13 November 2026.** Explains the blood route, external controller and power, transplant and long-term roles, one example of surgery with bypass, device education and risks. No page-level publication date was relied upon. [Johns Hopkins LVAD guide](https://www.hopkinsmedicine.org/health/treatment-tests-and-therapies/left-ventricular-assist-device)
