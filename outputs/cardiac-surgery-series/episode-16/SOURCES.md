# Clinical sources and scene map

**How a donor heart is connected in a heart transplant**

Elysian Biomedical LLP · Cardiac Surgery Education Series · Episode 16

Clinical sources checked on **13 November 2026**. The film presents one bicaval orthotopic transplant and a general account of lifelong follow-up.

## Dated references

- **A · ISHLT Guidelines for Evaluation and Care of Cardiac Transplant Candidates, published 8 August 2024 on the society site.** Supports assessment of candidates, care while waiting, and the individual medical decision. [ISHLT candidate guideline](https://www.ishlt.org/education-and-publications/standards-guidelines-detail/ishlt-guidelines-for-the-evaluation-and-care-of-cardiac-transplant-candidates)
- **B · NHS Blood and Transplant, Heart transplant surgery, accessed 13 November 2026.** Supports the donor heart’s usual position, cardiopulmonary bypass, the retained left atrial cuff and major vascular joins, and transition from bypass. No page-level publication date was displayed. [NHS Blood and Transplant surgical guide](https://www.nhsbt.nhs.uk/organ-transplantation/heart/at-the-transplant-centre/heart-transplant-surgery/)
- **C · Sarsam et al., An alternative surgical technique in orthotopic cardiac transplantation, May 1993.** Original description and clinical comparison of the approach preserving the donor right atrium. Use with the NHS Blood and Transplant anatomical guide for the five joins, not for modern outcome estimates. [Original surgical report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8507963/)
- **D · NHS Blood and Transplant, Rejection of a transplanted heart, accessed 13 November 2026.** Explains immunosuppression, rejection surveillance in well patients, sample testing and continuing clinical follow-up. No page-level publication date was displayed. [NHS Blood and Transplant rejection guide](https://www.nhsbt.nhs.uk/organ-transplantation/heart/benefits-and-risks-of-a-heart-transplant/risks-of-a-heart-transplant/rejection-of-a-transplanted-heart/)
- **E · NHS Blood and Transplant, How does the offering system work?, accessed 13 November 2026.** Supports blood group, size and individual clinical review as examples of factors when a donor heart is offered. Its allocation scheme describes one country. [NHS Blood and Transplant organ offering guide](https://www.nhsbt.nhs.uk/organ-transplantation/heart/receiving-a-heart/how-does-the-offering-system-work/)
- **F · NHS Blood and Transplant, Early risks of a heart transplant, accessed 13 November 2026.** Supports monitoring for bleeding and infection, possible early graft support and increased infection susceptibility from immunosuppression. [NHS Blood and Transplant early risks](https://www.nhsbt.nhs.uk/organ-transplantation/heart/benefits-and-risks-of-a-heart-transplant/risks-of-a-heart-transplant/early-risks-of-a-heart-transplant/)
