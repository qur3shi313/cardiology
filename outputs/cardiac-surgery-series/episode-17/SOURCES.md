## Dated references

- **A · American Heart Association, Atrial Septal Defect, reviewed 14 April 2026.** Covers the atrial wall, left-to-right flow example, right-heart and pulmonary effects, options for closure, patch materials and follow-up. [AHA patient and anatomy guide](https://www.heart.org/en/health-topics/congenital-heart-defects/about-congenital-heart-defects/atrial-septal-defect-asd)
- **B · Burkhart and Suri, Minimally invasive video assisted surgical closure of secundum atrial septal defect, January 2017.** Original operative report supports the right atrial view, cardiopulmonary bypass and inspection of repair. The narrated model abstracts the shared anatomy and patch concept from the case-specific details. [Original surgical report](https://www.annalscts.com/article/view/13330/html)
- **C · Hopkins et al., Surgical patch closure of atrial septal defects, June 2004.** Original surgical series supports closure with a patch and assessment for residual flow. Use the report for the surgical relationship and the checking principle. [Original patch closure study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15172284/)
- **D · 2025 ACC/AHA/HRS/ISACHD/SCAI guideline for adults with congenital heart disease, published online 18 December 2025.** Provides specialist adult follow-up and the decision framework when a significant secundum shunt coexists with pulmonary vascular disease. [AHA clinical guideline and takeaways](https://professional.heart.org/en/science-news/2025-guideline-for-the-management-of-adults-with-congenital-heart-disease/top-things-to-know)
