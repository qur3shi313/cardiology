# Clinical source map

**Complete repair of tetralogy of Fallot**

Elysian Biomedical LLP · Cardiac Surgery Education Series · Episode 19

References checked 13 November 2026. The case depicts a complete repair using a septal patch and transannular outflow patch. The depiction is an educational example of one operation.

## References

- **A · American Heart Association, Tetralogy of Fallot, reviewed 30 July 2026.** Defines all four features, explains the possible right-to-left shunt when pulmonary flow is obstructed, and describes complete repair and long-term follow-up. [AHA patient and family resource](https://www.heart.org/en/health-topics/congenital-heart-defects/about-congenital-heart-defects/tetralogy-of-fallot)
- **B · Children's Minnesota and The Children's Heart Clinic, Tetralogy of Fallot Repair, reviewed June 2023.** The treatment section identifies different levels of narrowing, septal patch closure, removal of obstructive muscle, enlargement across the valve ring and early recovery monitoring. Its illustrations are reference material only. [Institutional repair guide](https://www.childrensmn.org/Departments/cardiovascular/heart-illustrations/surgery/english/tofrepair.pdf)
- **C · American College of Cardiology congenital follow-up algorithm, updated 12 April 2026.** Covers oxygenation and anatomy in timing, anatomy-dependent repair, residual defects and lifelong surveillance. [ACC clinical practice algorithm](https://www.acc.org/Latest-in-Cardiology/Articles/2024/10/30/13/20/Clinical-Practice-Algorithm-For-the-Follow-Up-of-Unrepaired-and-Repaired-ToF)
- **D · Geva and colleagues, AHA scientific statement, published online 21 November 2024.** Supports pulmonary regurgitation, recurrent obstruction and rhythm surveillance after repair. [AHA scientific statement](https://professional.heart.org/en/science-news/long-term-management-of-right-ventricular-outflow-tract-dysfunction-in-repaired-tetralogy-of-fallot/top-things-to-know)

| P01 | Restricted pulmonary outflow may divert blood across the defect into the overriding aorta. Keep this direction specific to the illustrated case. The repair has a septal component and an outflow component. | A, B |
| P02 | The four features are a ventricular septal defect, overriding aorta, right ventricular outflow obstruction and right ventricular hypertrophy. Show one coherent sectional view. | A, B |
| P03 | Imaging, symptoms, oxygenation and individual anatomy guide treatment timing. Complete repair often occurs in infancy, with earlier intervention for some babies. The narration gives no timetable or threshold. | A–C |
| P04 | Bypass supports the operation. A patch closes the ventricular septal defect. The left ventricular route to the aorta remains open and the patch closes the path between chambers. | A, B |
| P05 | Removing obstructive muscle and enlarging the outlet across the pulmonary ring can relieve obstruction. Other anatomical configurations can permit preservation of the valve. | A–C |
| P06 | The intended routes are right ventricle to lungs and left ventricle to aorta. Preserve the aorta and valve positions between views. | A, B |
| P07 | Residual defects, outflow obstruction and pulmonary regurgitation are possible after repair. Record normal and possible leak inserts as separate states. | B–D |
| P08 | Lifelong surveillance may use echo, rhythm testing and cardiac MRI. Valve or outflow treatment can be discussed if dysfunction warrants it. | A, C, D |
