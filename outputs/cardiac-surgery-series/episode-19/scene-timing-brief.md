# Illustrated production storyboard

**Complete repair of tetralogy of Fallot**

Elysian Biomedical LLP · Cardiac Surgery Education Series · Episode 19

Planning runtime about **4:20**, including an eight-second source card. Retiming follows the approved Alexander recording. Use one simplified anterior teaching heart throughout. Distinguish the septal patch from the outflow patch by position and outline as well as colour. The [script](narration-script.md) and [source map](SOURCES.md) define the clinical scope.

| P02 · Four features | 0:28–0:57 | Sweep from the septal opening to the aortic valve straddling it. Pivot to the narrowed right ventricular outlet, then outline the thickened right ventricular wall. | P07 · Early checks | 2:50–3:18 | Move from monitoring beside a covered infant to three separate echo schematic inserts. | P08 · Lifelong care | 3:18–3:50 | Match the same repaired anatomy through childhood and adulthood, then reveal echo, rhythm trace and MRI slice as distinct checks. Keep arrows at the valve and right ventricle, with no claimed numerical result or fixed appointment interval. | **Lifelong congenital heart care** |
| P09 · Recap | 3:50–4:20 | Show two short repeats of the patches seating in their separate locations. Follow the two flow routes one last time, then hold the source card for eight seconds. | “Close the opening” · “Widen the outlet” · “Next: Arterial switch operation” |

Use original animation, the authentic Elysian logo, Josefin Sans and Fira Sans. Show only covered, anonymous people and use voice only. Reveal no more than two callouts together. Keep the pulmonary valve ring visible as a landmark in the patch shot. Do not turn the second patch into a plug, conduit or replacement valve.
