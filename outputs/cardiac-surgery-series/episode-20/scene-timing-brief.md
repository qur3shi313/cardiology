# Illustrated production storyboard

**Arterial switch operation for d-transposition of the great arteries**

Elysian Biomedical LLP · Cardiac Surgery Education Series · Episode 20

Planning runtime about **4:20**, including an eight-second source card. Retiming follows the approved Alexander recording. Use a fixed anterior heart view, with a side-view insert for great artery crossing. Identify roots by ventricle and upper vessels by destination. The [script](narration-script.md) and [source map](SOURCES.md) lock the example.
