# Coronary angiography review cuts

Episode 01A follows right radial access. Episode 01B follows right common femoral access using a micropuncture introducer and an exchange to a working sheath. Each is a standalone educational film with English synthetic narration, English captions and original schematic illustrations.

- [Femoral MP4](elysian-coronary-angiography-femoral.mp4) and [English captions](elysian-coronary-angiography-femoral.en.srt)

The wrist film shows one metal-needle approach. A short wire goes through the needle, the needle comes out over the wire, and the sheath and dilator go in together. The short wire and dilator come out before the longer diagnostic guidewire enters. A multipurpose catheter is repositioned between the coronary openings. [SCAI transradial guidance](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31880380/) · [Radial introducer instructions](https://www.terumoindiaskilllab.com/assets/pdf/09_04_IFU_GSS.pdf) · [Single-catheter angiography study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20627341/)

The groin film shows a fine access wire, a temporary coaxial introducer, a larger exchange wire, and then the working sheath. The working sheath remains at the groin when the left diagnostic catheter is exchanged for the right. [SCAI femoral access guidance](https://www.scai.org/quality-improvement-tools/qi-tips/safe-femoral-access-using-all-tools-toolbox) · [Micropuncture introducer instructions](https://ifu.cookmedical.com/data/IFU_PDF/T_MPIS_REV2.PDF) · [International societies’ transfemoral approach review](https://www.jacc.org/doi/10.1016/j.jcin.2017.08.035)

The source review also covered selective contrast, diagnostic findings, and wrist and groin aftercare. The animation represents the selected equipment and anatomy. A patient’s clinical team chooses the approach and discharge instructions. The caption files are timed to the narration by scene, with approximate word timing within each scene. [AHA coronary angiogram](https://www.heart.org/en/health-topics/heart-attack/diagnosing-a-heart-attack/coronary-angiogram) · [NHS angiogram aftercare](https://www.guysandstthomas.nhs.uk/health-information/coronary-angiogram/after-coronary-angiogram)
