# Video 3: Coronary angioplasty and stent placement

This is Episode 02 of the Elysian Biomedical LLP Cardiology Education Series. Production started after both revised angiogram videos went public: [1A, wrist access](https://youtu.be/YIw1bSATJqY) and [1B, groin access](https://youtu.be/sFbJGjFIuHo).

The next episode follows one illustrated example of coronary angioplasty. It moves from the diagnostic result to a treatment guidewire, balloon and stent, shows the stent staying in the artery after the balloon leaves, then returns to X-ray imaging and recovery. The example will distinguish treatment from diagnostic angiography and leave case-specific choices, medicines and follow-up to the care team.

Use the light Elysian illustration style, authentic logo, Josefin Sans and Fira Sans, and the approved Alexander English narration.
