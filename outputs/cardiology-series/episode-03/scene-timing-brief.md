# Coronary drug-coated balloons

Cardiology Intervention Series · Episode 03 · Video 4 · Elysian Biomedical LLP

## Clinical source check

- **P01, P04–P07.** FDA's 29 February 2024 original approval is specific to one product used after appropriate vessel preparation in selected adults with coronary in-stent restenosis. The PMA registry lists supplements. The episode names only the 2024 jurisdictional example and uses a generic device illustration. FDA's overview and original label describe the external drug coating, balloon contact and treatment mechanism. [FDA overview](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/recently-approved-devices/agent-paclitaxel-coated-balloon-catheter-p230035) · [FDA approval record and supplements](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpma/pma.cfm?ID=P230035)
- **P02–P04, P06.** SCAI distinguishes tissue growth, stent underexpansion and other causes, and recommends intracoronary imaging to understand the mechanism and guide treatment. The demonstration uses a tissue-growth example with a previously expanded stent. It preserves the original stent and shows adequate preparation before a drug-coated balloon. The approved FDA device's original labeling notes limited clinical evidence for bailout stenting with that specific device, so the narration describes alternative treatment generically, without modeling a specific rescue sequence. [SCAI expert consensus, 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11308135/) · [FDA original instructions for use](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf23/P230035C.pdf)
- **P03–P07.** The DCB Academic Research Consortium describes treatment selection, lesion preparation and post-treatment assessment. The Japanese CVIT consensus provides additional context on selected cases and research, including reasons to change the treatment plan after an inadequate result. Its jurisdiction-specific practice is separate from FDA labeling. [DCB-ARC position statement, 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41062231/) · [Japanese CVIT consensus, 2026](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12928-026-01357-0)
- **P07.** BASKET-SMALL 2 studied carefully selected previously unstented small coronary vessels after successful preparation. REC-CAGEFREE I tested a broader previously untreated population and did not demonstrate noninferiority of a DCB strategy against intended stenting. The script describes research in selected small vessels and leaves approvals and clinical selection to the relevant jurisdiction and clinician. [BASKET-SMALL 2 randomized trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30170854/) · [REC-CAGEFREE I original study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39236727/)
- **P08–P09.** Access-site closure and recovery depend on the route and case. Antiplatelet duration depends on the clinical context. Show serious symptoms without fixed medication or activity instructions. [Guy’s and St Thomas’ NHS recovery guidance, reviewed March 2026](https://www.guysandstthomas.nhs.uk/health-information/coronary-angioplasty/after-coronary-angioplasty) · [NHS urgent symptoms guidance](https://www.nhs.uk/tests-and-treatments/coronary-angioplasty/recovery/) · [Japanese CVIT consensus, 2026](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12928-026-01357-0)
