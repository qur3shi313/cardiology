# Clinical sources for transcatheter aortic valve implantation

Checked on 11 November 2026. The episode illustrates one transfemoral case using a balloon-expandable tissue valve in a narrowed native aortic valve.

| P01–P02 | The aortic valve opens from the left ventricle into the aorta. Stiff or calcified valve flaps restrict opening and can cause breathlessness, chest discomfort and fainting. | [AHA heart valves and circulation](https://www.heart.org/en/health-topics/heart-valve-problems-and-disease/about-heart-valves/) · [AHA aortic stenosis overview](https://www.heart.org/en/health-topics/heart-valve-problems-and-disease/heart-valve-problems-and-causes/problem-aortic-valve-stenosis) |

| P04–P06 | A transfemoral example uses the groin artery and follows the aorta to the aortic valve. The guidewire crosses into the left ventricle. In a balloon-expandable system, a folded delivery balloon carries the compressed valve. Balloon expansion seats the frame inside the original valve and displaces the native leaflets. The delivery balloon is deflated and removed. | [AHA TAVR overview](https://www.heart.org/en/health-topics/heart-valve-problems-and-disease/understanding-your-heart-valve-treatment-options/what-is-tavr) · [SCAI guidewire and delivery route](https://www.scai.org/quality-improvement-tools/qi-tips/how-deliver-transcatheter-aortic-valve-replacement-tavr-delivery) · [FDA-hosted SAPIEN 3 patient labeling, pp. 10–11](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf14/P140031S182C.pdf) · [Guy’s and St Thomas’ NHS TAVI procedure](https://www.guysandstthomas.nhs.uk/health-information/tavi-aortic-stenosis/tavi-procedure) |
| P07–P08 | Imaging checks valve function and flow around the frame. Staff close and check the groin access site and monitor heart rhythm. Bleeding, vessel injury, stroke and rhythm changes are recognized complications. A persistent rhythm problem can require a permanent pacemaker. | [FDA-hosted patient labeling, pp. 11–14](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf14/P140031S182C.pdf) · [University Hospital Southampton NHS post-TAVI care](https://www.uhs.nhs.uk/departments/blood-heart-and-circulation/transcatheter-aortic-valve-implantation-tavi/post-tavi-care) · [ACC TAVR rhythm and pacemaker pathway](https://www.acc.org/Latest-in-Cardiology/ten-points-to-remember/2020/10/20/20/59/2020-ACC-Expert-Consensus-Conduction-Disturbances) |

Regular follow-up checks valve function. | [Guy’s and St Thomas’ NHS TAVI recovery](https://www.guysandstthomas.nhs.uk/health-information/tavi-aortic-stenosis/recovery-tavi) · [Barts Health NHS TAVI wound care and follow-up](https://www.bartshealth.nhs.uk/tavi) · [AHA emergency symptoms](https://www.heart.org/en/about-us/heart-attack-and-stroke-symptoms) · [AHA when to call emergency services](https://www.heart.org/en/health-topics/house-calls/when-to-call-911) |

The ACC lists the 2020 ACC/AHA document as its current comprehensive valvular guideline and lists an update in development. In April 2025 the FDA expanded the indication for specified balloon-expandable devices to include selected patients with asymptomatic severe native calcific aortic stenosis when a Heart Team judges the treatment appropriate. The episode describes shared assessment and the procedure itself, so its explanation also applies when a team considers intervention before symptoms. [ACC guideline index](https://www.acc.org/guidelines) · [ACC clinical policy in progress](https://www.acc.org/guidelines/guidelines-and-clinical-documents-in-progress) · [FDA P140031/S182 approval record](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpma/pma.cfm?ID=P140031S182)

The procedure and recovery sources describe variations in valve design, access, monitoring and hospital stay. The illustrated balloon expansion represents the selected balloon-expandable device design. The patient script refers people to their own team for medicines, wound care, activity and driving guidance.
