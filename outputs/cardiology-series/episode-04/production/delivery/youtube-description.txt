How does a new heart valve reach the aortic valve through an artery in the groin? Video 5 of Elysian Biomedical LLP’s Cardiology Education Series follows one example of transcatheter aortic valve implantation (TAVI), also called transcatheter aortic valve replacement (TAVR).

See why aortic stenosis narrows the valve, how the Heart Team assesses treatment options, and how a balloon-expandable tissue valve opens inside the original valve. The animation also shows what the team checks after placement and what to expect during recovery and follow-up.

For general education. Your own care team can explain the treatment and discharge plan that apply to you. Original educational animation with simulated medical imaging and synthetic English narration.
