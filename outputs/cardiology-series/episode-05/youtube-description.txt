Why might the cath lab team use the wrist for one patient and the groin for another? Video 6 of Elysian Biomedical LLP’s Cardiology Education Series compares radial and femoral artery access for a coronary angiogram or catheter-based treatment (PCI).

See where each route starts, what can influence the choice, and why a team may change routes during a procedure. The episode also explains how staff control bleeding at each site, what can affect the time to sit up or walk, and when to get help after going home.

For general education. Your care team can explain the access choice and discharge plan that apply to you. Original educational animation with simulated medical imagery and synthetic English narration.
