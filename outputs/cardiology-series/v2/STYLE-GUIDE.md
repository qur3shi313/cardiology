# Visual and audio style

Open with a relevant visual action or question in the first five seconds. Use the authentic Elysian logo, Josefin Sans and Fira Sans, and the light green palette. Give the main artwork space to fill the frame, with strong contrast between the anatomy, devices, and labels. Use up to two callouts at a time. Size titles at 64 pixels or larger and labels at 40 pixels or larger at 1080p, with 34 pixels as the minimum for any text. Scale those sizes up proportionally for 1440p.

Keep the anatomy and camera steady while one device passes another, changes shape, or leaves the body. Camera movement can introduce a new scale or reveal a detail between actions. Show what moves and what stays fixed. Keep simulated imaging identifiable and retain the disclosures in the film and description.

Use original illustrated anatomy. People appear as fully and modestly clothed figures. The body route maps use sex-neutral silhouettes. For groin access, show the access site in a small opening in a surgical drape. Review the full timeline, thumbnail, and vertical cut for the owner's depiction rules.

Use Alexander's ElevenLabs English narration. Keep the source wording for retained claims and align captions to the actual recordings. Keep music out of all versions. Mix speech to −14 LUFS integrated with true peak at or below −1 dBTP. A short end card identifies the series, the next topic, and the clinical sources.

Deliver H.264 at 2560×1440 and 30 fps, plus a 1080p copy, English SRT, chapter list, 1280×720 thumbnail, vertical Short, and quality report. Check decoded frames on a phone-size contact sheet. Review readability, depiction, motion, anatomical continuity, and sync. Document intentional holds and use the frame checks to find unexpected static sections.
