## Reuse, framing and modesty

The old cutaways are useful for tracing needle trajectory, wall entry, lumen, device nesting and position. The original drawing canvas is 1600 × 900, while most delivered source MP4s are 1920 × 1080. The diagnostic-access devices and active window need native-resolution redraw with antialiasing. Keep the far wall, near-wall puncture, lumen, proximal hub or entry point, and moving distal tip in shot throughout a fixed-angle device action. Keep screen-space labels and subtitle safe areas independent of the moving anatomy. At 1080p use at least 64 px for titles, 40 px for labels and 34 px for other text. At 1440p use at least 86, 54 and 46 px respectively and measure actual glyph bounds. Reveal at most two labels at once. Place labels away from the catheter tip and clear space for captions and end-screen links.

- **Wrist cutaway.** In legacy 1600 × 900 coordinates the main section lies roughly within `x=100–1535, y=325–710`. The active shaft extends above that section in several stages. Preserve at least `y≈225–710` as visual reference and trace the complete device bounds at first, middle and last frames. This wide strip has an aspect ratio near 3:1. Filling 16:9 with a cover crop would cut away ends of instruments or the artery. Extract/repaint the anatomy and extend surgical drape or surrounding field to 16:9. Use a closer detail only after the full entry/hub/tip relationship is established. Use a right palm with correct thumb-side radial location. `episode-05/visuals/render_access_comparison.py` has the later right-palm locator; its existing backdrop still needs the v2 layout.
- **Groin cutaway.** The old `groin_locator()` in `femoral-proof/render_femoral_proof.py` draws a flesh-colored pelvis with a navel and contour marks around `x=1297–1495, y=208–327`. That locator is baked into multiple legacy views. Replace it at source with opaque surgical drape and a small single right common femoral puncture window. Reuse only the device path and internal vessel geometry. The flattened groin cutaway spans about `x=102–1516, y=325–709`, the same crop used in `shared-coronary-scenes/render_shared_coronary.py` for its groin inset. It can supply a tracing plate for the tissue and catheter states. A bottom-only crop may cut off the external hub or moving approach shaft. Mask or repaint above the puncture before enlarging, and keep hips, upper thigh contours and the neighboring body surface under drape throughout the camera move.
- **Routes and recovery.** Redraw R12 and F16’s skin-toned body outlines as neutral cloth silhouettes without external features. Preserve internal geometry from `render_radial_scenes.py` and `render_route.py`, and keep the right-side marker visible. F27’s old `render_recovery.py:143` illustration depicts uncovered leg and hair. Use a fully covered neutral figure, or a tight view of dressing, gloved checks and monitor. F26 can use a socked/covered foot with a clear circulation-check cue. Do not include the bare leg/hair or old groin locator in the long film, Short, thumbnail, contact sheet or end card assets.
- **Root and coronary views.** Reuse the verified path parameters and independent vessel masks from `shared-coronary-scenes/render_shared_coronary.py`. Its flattened left/right grayscale film is only `x=800–1490, y=324–739`, about 20% of the old frame. A direct enlarge would raster-scale a small image and carry embedded small labels or clip branch edges. Render a new fullscreen grayscale projection with long branches, clear dark-on-light contrast, mild motion/texture and a new `Simulated fluoroscopy` disclosure. Keep the original field of view inside the larger picture. For the root, use one clear large cutaway, with the coronary origin and valve in the same frame; move from the body map to the aortic root and then settle the camera for seating. Preserve separate flow events and labels for left and right runs.
- **Modesty review.** Use original anatomical and equipment illustrations only. Inspect the full film, every transition edge, and the alternate crop for each moving scene. Review the 390 px-wide, five-second sampling grid plus entry, exit and peak visibility frames between samples. Trace the camera’s full bounds to ensure no crop reveals an old locator or unmasked body. Report actual checks in `QA.json` after rendering.
