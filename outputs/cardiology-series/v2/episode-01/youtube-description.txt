How does a cardiology team reach the heart’s arteries through the wrist or groin? This illustrated episode follows both routes, from the initial access needle and guidewire to the sheath, diagnostic catheter, contrast injection and X-ray images. It also covers what the images can show, how each access site is closed, and what the team checks during recovery.
