# Clinical source map for the tools of a coronary angiogram

Source check on 11 November 2026. The episode follows a right radial example for a catheter-based diagnostic coronary angiogram, with a separate common femoral example. The device references support how the illustrated components fit together. The film gives a patient overview and omits device setup and operator instructions.

| Scenes | Evidence used | Sources |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P01–P02 | Diagnostic coronary angiography uses an arterial catheter, contrast and X-rays to show the arteries supplying the heart. The catheter can enter through an artery near the wrist or groin. The sheath remains at the entry site and the catheter travels to the heart. | [American Heart Association coronary angiogram](https://www.heart.org/en/health-topics/heart-attack/diagnosing-a-heart-attack/coronary-angiogram) · [Frimley Health NHS coronary angiogram leaflet](https://www.fhft.nhs.uk/patients-and-visitors/patient-information-library/having-coronary-angiogram-fph) |
| P03–P04 | The illustrated radial introducer has a needle, a short guidewire and a sheath with a tapered dilator. After access, the needle comes out while the short wire remains. Sheath and dilator go in together over that wire. The dilator and short wire come out, leaving the sheath as a passage for a catheter or catheter exchange. | [Radial introducer instructions, English steps 1–14](https://www.terumoindiaskilllab.com/assets/pdf/09_04_IFU_GSS.pdf) · [SCAI transradial best-practice update](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31880380/) |
| P05 | The common femoral example uses micropuncture access. A fine wire supports a temporary coaxial introducer. A separate exchange wire can support placement of the working sheath. This describes the selected example. Other equipment and approaches exist. | [SCAI safe femoral access guidance](https://www.scai.org/quality-improvement-tools/qi-tips/safe-femoral-access-using-all-tools-toolbox) · [Micropuncture introducer instructions, English illustrations and steps](https://ifu.cookmedical.com/data/IFU_PDF/T_MPIS_REV2.PDF) · [International transfemoral access practice survey](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29169496/) |
| P06–P07 | In the illustrated diagnostic sequence, the longer guidewire supports catheter advancement under live X-ray. The guidewire is withdrawn, leaving the catheter in the aorta before selective contrast. The curved catheter tip is positioned at a coronary opening. The source describing early selective transfemoral technique supports this relationship, and contemporary studies and guidance document radial diagnostic catheter choices. | [AHA transradial coronary angiography scientific statement, catheter selection and advancement](https://www.ahajournals.org/doi/10.1161/HCV.0000000000000035) · [Original selective coronary arteriography study, Judkins](https://pubs.rsna.org/doi/10.1148/89.5.815) · [Diagnostic angiographic catheter manufacturer information](https://www.bostonscientific.com/en-US/products/catheters--diagnostic/expo-diagnostic-catheter/expo-prescriptive-information.html) · [Kane et al., 2026 randomized comparison of diagnostic transradial catheter strategies](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42031154/) |
| P08–P09 | Contrast travels through the diagnostic catheter and outlines the coronary lumen during flow. A series of X-rays is recorded during passage and the camera can obtain views from different angles. Selective injections at each coronary opening record the corresponding arterial tree. The washout animation reflects contrast moving on with circulating blood. | [NHS angiography procedure](https://www.nhs.uk/tests-and-treatments/angiography/what-happens/) · [Frimley Health NHS coronary angiogram leaflet](https://www.fhft.nhs.uk/patients-and-visitors/patient-information-library/having-coronary-angiogram-fph) · [AHA coronary angiogram](https://www.heart.org/en/health-topics/heart-attack/diagnosing-a-heart-attack/coronary-angiogram) · [Original selective coronary arteriography study](https://pubs.rsna.org/doi/10.1148/89.5.815) |
| P10 | Intra-arterial iodinated contrast is used for selective coronary angiography. The team considers pre-existing kidney problems and past reactions to contrast. Brief warmth can occur. In-procedure chest discomfort, itching or trouble breathing warrants telling the team immediately. The script uses no absolute risk estimate, individual contrast plan, preparation timeline or medicine instruction. | [AHA evidence-based practices in the cath lab](https://professional.heart.org/en/science-news/evidence-based-practices-in-the-cardiac-catheterization-laboratory/top-things-to-know) · [ACC/AHA/SCAI revascularization guideline, best practices for angiography in CKD, Table 9](https://www.heart.org/-/media/PHD-Files-2/Science-News/2/2021/2021-Coronary-Artery-Revascularization-Guideline-Slide-Set.pdf) · [Leeds Teaching Hospitals coronary angiography patient guidance](https://www.leedsth.nhs.uk/patients/resources/understanding-coronary-angiography-and-coronary-intervention/) · [NHS angiography risks](https://www.nhs.uk/tests-and-treatments/angiography/risks/) · [ACR Manual on Contrast Media](https://www.acr.org/Clinical-Resources/Clinical-Tools-and-Reference/Contrast-Manual) · [DailyMed iodinated contrast label, indications and safety](https://www.dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=798f3bc6-d976-49a6-87a2-fd30c784fc50) |
| P11–P12 | The catheter and sheath are removed after a completed diagnostic test. Staff manage bleeding and check the access site. The cardiologist reviews the recorded X-ray images. | [AHA coronary angiogram](https://www.heart.org/en/health-topics/heart-attack/diagnosing-a-heart-attack/coronary-angiogram) · [Radial introducer instructions, English step 14](https://www.terumoindiaskilllab.com/assets/pdf/09_04_IFU_GSS.pdf) · [Guy’s and St Thomas’ NHS angiogram recovery](https://www.guysandstthomas.nhs.uk/health-information/coronary-angiogram/after-coronary-angiogram) |
