## Visual and clinical continuity

- Use a full-bleed moving coronary tree and cutaway views. Keep one focal LAD lesion beyond the left main division consistent across projections. Reserve clear space for no more than two labels at once and distinguish illustrative grayscale imaging from anatomical color views.
- Depict arteries, organs, tools and fully clothed modest figures as original artwork. Use sex-neutral silhouettes for location cues and no real patient images. Use the authentic logo, website fonts and narration without music. Show truthful simulation and synthetic-narration disclosure.
- Keep a continuous vessel path and stable anatomy. Preserve the stationary lesion and the same branch landmarks while moving the camera. Keep treatment options neutral and distinguish the pressure-wire concept from the angiogram. Avoid device settings, clinical thresholds, doses and individualized recommendations.
