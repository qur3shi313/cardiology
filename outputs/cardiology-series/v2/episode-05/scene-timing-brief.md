## Visual and clinical continuity

- Prefer full-bleed original visuals of hands, equipment and the small access site. Use a modestly clothed person or sex-neutral silhouette if needed. The groin stays draped with only a small access window, and diagrams show internal anatomy without nudity. Use narration without music.
- Keep the right wrist and right common femoral examples separate and anatomically consistent. Use a right palm-up position with the thumb on the correct side. The femoral access sits above the common femoral artery division. Show staff checks and closure at a general education level.
- Distinguish planned diagnostic angiography, PCI and acute heart attack care. Show examples of individualized discharge advice without including treatment instructions, drug names, doses, durations or numerical thresholds. Keep care-team contact and emergency alert actions distinct.
- Reveal no more than two short labels at a time. Check legibility at 390 px and inspect all frames of each closure and safety scene. Use the authentic logo, website fonts and truthful production disclosure. [Clinical sources](SOURCES.md)
