# Coronary angioplasty and stenting

The angioplasty film illustrates a representative balloon-expandable coronary stent in an original artery cutaway. The guidewire crosses the narrowed channel first. A collapsed stent travels on a folded balloon over the wire. The balloon opens the stent against the artery wall, then deflates before it is withdrawn. The wire leaves separately, and the mesh stays at the treated site. [American Heart Association, What is a Stent?](https://www.heart.org/en/health-topics/heart-attack/treatment-of-a-heart-attack/stent) · [FDA-hosted coronary stent labeling, delivery, deployment and removal](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf11/P110019S115D.pdf#page=118)

The cutaway shows one selected case and compresses time for education. Preparation of a narrowing and final checks vary with the artery, patient and equipment. The expanded mesh illustrates how a stent helps support the treated segment. [American Heart Association, Cardiac Procedures and Surgeries](https://www.heart.org/en/health-topics/heart-attack/treatment-of-a-heart-attack/cardiac-procedures-and-surgeries)
