# How drug-eluting stents work

Elysian Biomedical LLP · Cardiology Education Series · v2 Video 7 · Approved topic 07

Checked on 11 November 2026. The evidence letters match the storyboard. Clinical wording is paraphrased for a general audience.

- **A** Supports the metal scaffold, coronary drug-eluting coating, and its role in reducing the chance of re-narrowing. [NIH NHLBI — What are stents?, updated November 2023](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/stents)
- **B** Supports placement by balloon expansion and leaving a stent at the treated coronary segment. [American Heart Association — What is a stent?](https://www.heart.org/en/health-topics/heart-attack/treatment-of-a-heart-attack/stent)
- **C** Supports use of drug-eluting stents, intravascular imaging in selected settings and individualized antiplatelet care. [2021 ACC/AHA/SCAI coronary revascularization guideline, sections 10.2, 10.3 and 14.2](https://www.ahajournals.org/doi/pdf/10.1161/CIR.0000000000001038)
- **D** Supports a balloon carrying medication on its exterior and use of this specific device for eligible in-stent restenosis. This is a US product-label example, without a general claim about approvals or eligibility. [FDA — Coronary drug-coated balloon approval overview, original 2024 approval](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/recently-approved-devices/agent-paclitaxel-coated-balloon-catheter-p230035)
- **E** Supports regular follow-up, ongoing coronary disease care, and the purpose of prescribed antiplatelet medicines in reducing clot risk. [NIH NHLBI — Living with a stent, updated November 2023](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/stents/living-with)
- **F** Supports clinician-selected antiplatelet treatment, including consideration of bleeding risk, and selected use of intracoronary imaging. No specific duration or regimen is stated. [2025 ACC/AHA/ACEP/NAEMSP/SCAI guideline for acute coronary syndromes](https://www.ahajournals.org/doi/pdf/10.1161/CIR.0000000000001309)
- **G** Checks the nested device relationship, stent attached to the delivery balloon and deflation before withdrawal. The narration and artwork use generic geometry and omit the product name, device settings and inflation medium. [FDA-hosted device instructions for a balloon-expandable coronary drug-eluting stent](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf7/P070015S128D.pdf)
- **H** Supports the post-treatment contrast check. This is established procedural education. The listed review due date has passed, and current guidance on selected inside-artery imaging is covered by C and F. [NHS — Coronary angioplasty and stent insertion, how it is performed](https://www.nhs.uk/tests-and-treatments/coronary-angioplasty/what-happens/)
