# Coronary drug-coated balloons: clinical sources

This film uses a generic coronary in-stent restenosis example. The device illustrations are original. References to regulatory decisions are limited to their named jurisdiction and device. The film makes no claim about availability, local approval, suitability for a particular patient or an Elysian Biomedical product.

- P01–P03: [SCAI expert consensus on in-stent restenosis and stent thrombosis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11308135/) (2023). Supports distinct mechanisms, imaging, why a repeat narrowing is evaluated and treatment decisions.
- P04–P08: [FDA AGENT decision and labeling](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpma/pma.cfm?ID=P230035) (2024) and [FDA summary of safety and effectiveness](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf23/P230035B.pdf). A selected device is an example for indication, vessel preparation, drug transfer, full deflation and removal. Do not copy settings, device dimensions or labeling as general instructions. The original stent remains; this balloon does not implant another scaffold.
- P09: [NHS coronary angioplasty recovery](https://www.nhs.uk/tests-and-treatments/coronary-angioplasty/recovery/) for general post-PCI recovery and following the treating team’s plan. Recovery and medicine instructions vary.
