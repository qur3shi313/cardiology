# Why a stent can narrow again and how the team investigates

Elysian Biomedical LLP · Cardiology Education Series · v2 Video 9 · Approved topic 09

Checked on 11 November 2026. The evidence letters match the storyboard. Clinical wording is paraphrased for a general audience.

- **C** Supports use of drug-eluting stents, intravascular imaging in selected settings and individualized antiplatelet care. [2021 ACC/AHA/SCAI coronary revascularization guideline, sections 10.2, 10.3 and 14.2](https://www.ahajournals.org/doi/pdf/10.1161/CIR.0000000000001038)
- **D** Supports a balloon carrying medication on its exterior and use of this specific device for eligible in-stent restenosis. This is a US product-label example, without a general claim about approvals or eligibility. [FDA — Coronary drug-coated balloon approval overview, original 2024 approval](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/recently-approved-devices/agent-paclitaxel-coated-balloon-catheter-p230035)
- **E** Supports regular follow-up, ongoing coronary disease care, and the purpose of prescribed antiplatelet medicines in reducing clot risk. [NIH NHLBI — Living with a stent, updated November 2023](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/stents/living-with)
- **H** Supports causes of recurrent narrowing, the distinction from stent thrombosis, role of angiography and inside-artery imaging, and mechanism-specific treatment. [SCAI expert consensus on in-stent restenosis and stent thrombosis, 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11308135/)
- **I** Supports the distinction between stent thrombosis and recurrent narrowing, investigation, correction of mechanical causes and possible drug-coated balloon or repeat drug-eluting stent treatment. [2024 ESC guideline for chronic coronary syndromes, section 6.4](https://doi.org/10.1093/eurheartj/ehae177)
- **J** Supports emergency help for possible heart attack symptoms. The narration uses a location-neutral emergency action. [American Heart Association — Heart attack and stroke symptoms](https://www.heart.org/en/about-us/heart-attack-and-stroke-symptoms)
- **K** Supports contrast imaging of the coronary lumen and using findings to discuss treatment options. [American Heart Association — Coronary angiogram](https://www.heart.org/en/health-topics/heart-attack/diagnosing-a-heart-attack/coronary-angiogram)
