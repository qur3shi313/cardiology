## Visual and clinical continuity

- Use full-bleed original motion art and the authentic Elysian logo with website fonts. Prefer tools, organs, drapes, hands and sex-neutral silhouettes. Use fully and modestly clothed figures if visible. Use narration without music.
- Keep no more than two readable callouts on screen and show clinically relevant movement rather than arbitrary camera drift. Check the 390 px phone preview and inspect all frames at key device transitions. Disclose simulated imagery and synthetic narration.
- Preserve the original stent in the focal narrowing. Illustrate underexpansion, tissue growth, later plaque and acute clot as separate possibilities. Clinical symptoms alone do not establish their cause.
