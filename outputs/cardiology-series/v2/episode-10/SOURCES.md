# Recovery, medicines and follow-up after PCI

Elysian Biomedical LLP · Cardiology Education Series · v2 Video 10 · Approved topic 10

Checked on 11 November 2026. Evidence IDs match the scene table. Claims are paraphrased for general education.

- **A** Supports in-hospital checks, access closure care, case-dependent discharge, activity guidance, follow-up and rehab. [Guy’s and St Thomas’ NHS Foundation Trust — After coronary angioplasty, reviewed March 2026](https://www.guysandstthomas.nhs.uk/health-information/coronary-angioplasty/after-coronary-angioplasty)
- **B** Supports ongoing care, clot risk and clinician direction on prescribed medicines and activity. [NHLBI — Living with a stent, updated November 2023](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/stents/living-with)
- **C** Supports antiplatelet therapy tailored to the clinical situation and bleeding risk, cardiac rehabilitation and ongoing secondary prevention after acute coronary syndrome. [2025 ACC/AHA/ACEP/NAEMSP/SCAI guideline for acute coronary syndromes](https://www.ahajournals.org/doi/pdf/10.1161/CIR.0000000000001309)
- **D** Supports rehabilitation where eligible, routine review of symptoms, medications and risk factors, and context-dependent follow-up testing. [2023 AHA/ACC multisociety guideline for chronic coronary disease, sections 4.2.10 and 7.1](https://www.ahajournals.org/doi/pdf/10.1161/CIR.0000000000001168)
- **E** Supports discussing procedures and any possible antiplatelet interruption with the clinician and care team. The episode uses generic education without product promotion. [FDA-hosted patient guide for a coronary drug-eluting stent](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf7/P070015S122d.pdf)
- **F** Supports general wound warning signs and prompt emergency care for serious chest pain, persistent bleeding or changes in limb color, warmth or feeling. Its listed review due date has passed. A and G corroborate the general principles used here. [NHS — Coronary angioplasty and stent insertion, recovery](https://www.nhs.uk/tests-and-treatments/coronary-angioplasty/recovery/)
- **G** Supports chest pressure and shortness of breath as emergency warning signs. Use location-neutral emergency wording. [American Heart Association — Heart attack and stroke symptoms](https://www.heart.org/en/about-us/heart-attack-and-stroke-symptoms)
