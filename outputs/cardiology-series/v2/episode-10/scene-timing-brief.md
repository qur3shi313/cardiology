## Visual and clinical continuity

- Use original full-bleed anatomy and device artwork, the authentic Elysian logo and website fonts. Show modestly clothed, simplified figures or hands and tools. Keep groin examples draped and use no real patient imagery. Use narration without music.
- Keep diagnostic-only care distinct from treatment with a stent. Show clot prevention, dressing and activity guidance, and ongoing coronary disease care as separate topics. Emergency symptoms are a separate beat. Omit named drugs, doses, fixed medicine duration, transport instructions or personal eligibility.
