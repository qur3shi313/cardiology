# How FFR and iFR help assess a coronary narrowing

Elysian Biomedical LLP · Cardiology Education Series · v2 Video 11 · Approved topic 11

Checked on 11 November 2026. Evidence IDs match the scene table. Claims are paraphrased for general education.

- **H** Supports using coronary physiology for selected decisions and checking the pressure signal. The episode omits equipment setup, thresholds and procedure instructions. [2025 AHA scientific statement on invasive epicardial coronary physiological assessment](https://professional.heart.org/en/science-news/evidence-based-practices-in-the-cardiac-catheterization-laboratory-2025/top-things-to-know)
- **I** Supports comparing angiographic appearance with FFR or iFR for selected intermediate lesions and placing results in a clinical treatment decision. [2021 ACC/AHA/SCAI guideline for coronary revascularization, sections 4.1 and 4.3](https://www.jacc.org/doi/full/10.1016/j.jacc.2021.09.006)
- **J** Supports the distal coronary to aortic pressure relationship, FFR under maximal hyperemia and iFR measured at rest during a selected part of diastole. [2020 AHA/ACC coronary revascularization data standards, FFR and iFR definitions](https://www.ahajournals.org/doi/pdf/10.1161/HCQ.0000000000000059)
- **K** Supports separating angiographic degree of narrowing from physiological significance and considering the clinical context and lesion anatomy. [2024 ESC chronic coronary syndromes guideline, section 3.3.3.2](https://doi.org/10.1093/eurheartj/ehae177)
- **L** Corroborates FFR and iFR as different pressure-based strategies. The script makes no trial outcome, universal equivalence or cutoff claim. [DEFINE-FLAIR randomized trial, original publication in NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1700445)
- **M** Supports an angiogram showing the outline of the coronary lumen and using results to discuss different care pathways. [American Heart Association — Coronary angiogram](https://www.heart.org/en/health-topics/heart-attack/diagnosing-a-heart-attack/coronary-angiogram)
