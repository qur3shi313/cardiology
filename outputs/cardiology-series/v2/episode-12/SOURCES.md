# Clinical evidence · Looking inside coronary arteries with IVUS and OCT

Public-education source review · 11 November 2026

## A · 2025 ACC/AHA/ACEP/NAEMSP/SCAI Acute Coronary Syndromes Guideline

Section 7.3 supports IVUS or OCT to guide PCI in ACS patients with complex lesions and describes roles in preparation, sizing and optimisation. [2025 ACC/AHA/ACEP/NAEMSP/SCAI Acute Coronary Syndromes Guideline](https://www.ahajournals.org/doi/10.1161/CIR.0000000000001309).

## B · EAPCI expert consensus on clinical use of intracoronary imaging

Parts on image acquisition, modality selection, plaque assessment and stent optimisation support ultrasound versus optical imaging, blood clearance, depth, resolution, expansion and apposition. [EAPCI expert consensus on clinical use of intracoronary imaging](https://academic.oup.com/eurheartj/article/39/35/3281/5001185).

## C · 2024 SCAI Expert Consensus Statement on Calcified Coronary Lesions

The imaging section supports ultrasound calcium with acoustic shadowing and use of IVUS and OCT to characterise coronary calcification. [2024 SCAI Expert Consensus Statement on Calcified Coronary Lesions](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11307856/).
