# Clinical evidence · Heart attack and emergency PCI

Public-education source review · 11 November 2026

## A · 2025 ACC/AHA/ACEP/NAEMSP/SCAI Acute Coronary Syndromes Guideline

The assessment, STEMI reperfusion, PCI, other coronary lesions and discharge sections support the care sequence, roles of ECG and troponin, prompt primary PCI when available, and tailored follow-up. [2025 ACC/AHA/ACEP/NAEMSP/SCAI Acute Coronary Syndromes Guideline](https://www.ahajournals.org/doi/10.1161/CIR.0000000000001309).

## B · American Heart Association Heart Attack and Stroke Symptoms

Official patient guidance supports recognition of possible warning signs and calling local emergency services promptly. The video omits the US-specific phone number. [American Heart Association Heart Attack and Stroke Symptoms](https://www.heart.org/en/about-us/heart-attack-and-stroke-symptoms).

## C · 2024 SCAI Expert Consensus Statement on Patients With STEMI Referred for Primary PCI

The early-care and cath-lab sections support identifying and treating the infarct-related artery and assessing flow after intervention. [2024 SCAI Expert Consensus Statement on Patients With STEMI Referred for Primary PCI](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11624394/).

## D · AHA Key Patient Messages for the 2025 ACS Guideline

Patient guidance supports discharge education, prescribed treatment, referral to cardiac rehabilitation and follow-up. [AHA Key Patient Messages for the 2025 ACS Guideline](https://professional.heart.org/en/science-news/patient-resources/key-patient-messages-2025-acute-coronary-syndromes-guideline).
