# Clinical evidence · Treatment decisions when several coronary arteries are narrowed

Public-education source review · 11 November 2026

## A · 2021 ACC/AHA/SCAI Guideline for Coronary Artery Revascularization

Heart Team and shared decision recommendations, coronary physiology and revascularization guidance, and diabetes and multivessel recommendations support the factors and example choices. [2021 ACC/AHA/SCAI Guideline for Coronary Artery Revascularization](https://professional.heart.org/en/science-news/2021-acc-aha-scai-guideline-for-coronary-artery-revascularization).

## B · 2023 AHA/ACC et al. Guideline for Chronic Coronary Disease

Medical therapy, risk management and revascularization recommendations support ongoing prevention, symptom goals and selected treatment in stable disease. [2023 AHA/ACC et al. Guideline for Chronic Coronary Disease](https://professional.heart.org/en/guidelines-statements/2023-ahaaccaccpaspcnlapcna-guideline-for-the-management-of-patients-withcir0000000000001168).

## C · 2025 ACC/AHA/ACEP/NAEMSP/SCAI Acute Coronary Syndromes Guideline

The multivessel ACS sections support treatment of the culprit artery first in appropriate emergency pathways and contextual plans for other significant lesions. [2025 ACC/AHA/ACEP/NAEMSP/SCAI Acute Coronary Syndromes Guideline](https://www.ahajournals.org/doi/10.1161/CIR.0000000000001309).

## D · AHA Cardiac Procedures and Surgeries

AHA describes a coronary bypass graft as an alternate route carrying blood around the diseased artery segment. [AHA Cardiac Procedures and Surgeries](https://www.heart.org/en/health-topics/heart-attack/treatment-of-a-heart-attack/cardiac-procedures-and-surgeries).
