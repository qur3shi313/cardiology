# Clinical evidence · How the Heart Team plans TAVI

Public-education source review · 11 November 2026

## A · 2025 ESC/EACTS Guidelines for the Management of Valvular Heart Disease

Section 8 imaging and intervention planning supports the roles of echocardiography and cardiac CT, annulus and root measurement, coronary access considerations, calcium and arterial route, and the Heart Team decision. [2025 ESC/EACTS Guidelines for the Management of Valvular Heart Disease](https://academic.oup.com/eurheartj/article/46/44/4635/8234488).

## B · Guy’s and St Thomas’ NHS Foundation Trust TAVI Assessment

Reviewed April 2025. Describes the optional assessment tests, multidisciplinary review, patient consent and discussion, and referral to surgery or further tests when appropriate. [Guy’s and St Thomas’ NHS Foundation Trust TAVI Assessment](https://www.guysandstthomas.nhs.uk/health-information/tavi-aortic-stenosis/tavi-assessment).
