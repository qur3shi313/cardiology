# Clinical evidence · Recovery and follow-up after TAVI

Public-education source review · 11 November 2026

## A · Guy’s and St Thomas’ NHS Foundation Trust Recovery from a TAVI

Discharge, wound, medication, activity, warning signs and follow-up sections support the patient-facing scope. Site-specific timing and medicine guidance are omitted. [Guy’s and St Thomas’ NHS Foundation Trust Recovery from a TAVI](https://www.guysandstthomas.nhs.uk/health-information/tavi-aortic-stenosis/recovery-tavi).

## B · University Hospital Southampton NHS Post-TAVI Care

In-hospital observations, rhythm and valve checks, variation in length of stay and follow-up echocardiography inform the recovery pathway. [University Hospital Southampton NHS Post-TAVI Care](https://www.uhs.nhs.uk/departments/blood-heart-and-circulation/transcatheter-aortic-valve-implantation-tavi/post-tavi-care).

## C · University Hospitals Sussex NHS TAVI Discharge Advice

Supports access-site emergency bleeding, stroke warnings and emergency response, wound checks and recovery planning. The emergency number is localised generically. [University Hospitals Sussex NHS TAVI Discharge Advice](https://www.uhsussex.nhs.uk/resources/discharge-advice-for-same-day-discharge-transcatheter-aortic-valve-implantation-tavi-at-the-sussex-cardiac-centre/).

## D · 2025 ESC/EACTS Guidelines for the Management of Valvular Heart Disease

Sections 8.5 and 14 describe conduction complications, pacemaker consideration, paravalvular leak and prosthetic-valve monitoring. [2025 ESC/EACTS Guidelines for the Management of Valvular Heart Disease](https://academic.oup.com/eurheartj/article/46/44/4635/8234488).

## E · NHS Recovering from a Heart Valve Replacement

Explains wound types, gradual activity, individual recovery and follow-up after valve replacement, including TAVI. [NHS Recovering from a Heart Valve Replacement](https://www.nhs.uk/tests-and-treatments/heart-valve-replacement/recovery/).
