# Clinical evidence · TAVI, surgery and how the Heart Team weighs the options

Public-education source review · 11 November 2026

## A · 2025 ESC/EACTS Guidelines for the Management of Valvular Heart Disease

Sections 8.5.1–8.5.2 and 14.1 support choice of treatment modality, anatomy and concomitant disease considerations, lifetime management, and prosthesis choice. [2025 ESC/EACTS Guidelines for the Management of Valvular Heart Disease](https://academic.oup.com/eurheartj/article/46/44/4635/8234488).

## B · American Heart Association What Is TAVR? (TAVI)

Explains the catheter route, expansion inside the existing valve, expected recovery and example procedural risks, and possible choice of surgery. [American Heart Association What Is TAVR? (TAVI)](https://www.heart.org/en/health-topics/heart-valve-problems-and-disease/understanding-your-heart-valve-treatment-options/what-is-tavr).

## C · American Heart Association Heart Valve Replacement Options

Supports tissue and mechanical options and factors that shape the decision. [American Heart Association Heart Valve Replacement Options](https://www.heart.org/en/health-topics/heart-valve-problems-and-disease/understanding-your-heart-valve-treatment-options/options-for-heart-valve-replacement).

## D · NHS Recovering from a Heart Valve Replacement

Supports different early recovery courses after transcatheter and surgical procedures. The narration gives no fixed duration. [NHS Recovering from a Heart Valve Replacement](https://www.nhs.uk/tests-and-treatments/heart-valve-replacement/recovery/).

## E · American Heart Association Cardiac Procedures and Surgeries

Supports the separate CABG bridge describing a graft carrying blood around a coronary narrowing. [American Heart Association Cardiac Procedures and Surgeries](https://www.heart.org/en/health-topics/heart-attack/treatment-of-a-heart-attack/cardiac-procedures-and-surgeries).
